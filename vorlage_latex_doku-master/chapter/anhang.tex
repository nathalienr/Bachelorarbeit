\chapter{Detailed Requirement Derivation Matrices}
\label{app:requirement_derivation}


This appendix provides the requirement-by-requirement documentation underlying the derivation methodology described in Section~\ref{sec:security_requirements}. It contains the complete catalogue of security requirements derived from Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, Annex III, together with their detailed mapping to prEN 50742 and, subsequently, to IEC 62443-4-2 Component Requirements (CR), Requirement Enhancements (RE), and component-type-specific instantiations such as Embedded Device Requirements (EDR). The three tables in this appendix correspond directly to the three derivation stages discussed in Section~\ref{sec:security_requirements} and share a common requirement identifier (RQ-XXX) to preserve traceability across all stages.

Table~\ref{tab:pren50742-mapping} maps the security requirements of Table~\ref{tab:mvo-requirements} onto the corresponding clauses, concepts, or topics of prEN 50742:2025 (\emph{Safety of machinery - Protection against corruption}). The mapping is restricted to Approach A (Sections 4, 5, 7 of prEN 50742) and Annex ZZ, since these are the only parts of the source text that provide clause-level content. Approach B (Sections 6, 8) is referenced only where it explicitly names a target standard/clause.

Each row records one of four mapping types: 
\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Direct}, where prEN 50742 contains a clause expressing essentially the same obligation
	\item \textbf{Partial}, where prEN 50742 covers part of the obligation but the scope, trigger condition, or wording differs
	\item \textbf{Indirect}, where prEN 50742 addresses a related concept or general principle without a clause-level match
	\item \textbf{Not identified}, where no supporting clause could be found, representing a confirmed gap or, where noted, a conflict
\end{itemize}

IEC 62443-4-2 organizes its Component Requirements under the seven Foundational Requirements introduced in Section~\ref{subsec:iec_62443}. In addition to Direct and Partial, this table uses the mapping types: 
\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Supporting}: a control that reduces but does not close the gap between a security-driven CR and a broader, non-security-specific MVO obligation
	\item \textbf{No direct mapping}: no CR exists, e.g. for process-level or purely functional-safety obligations, 
	\item \textbf{Candidate for direct MVO-to-IEC mapping}: a conceptually relevant CR that cannot be reached through the prEN 50742 derivation chain because prEN 50742 does not cover the underlying obligation 
\end{itemize}
